## Caveats

- Analytic power assumes normal outcomes with equal SDs. With equal group sizes, Welch's degrees of freedom then equal Student's (2n − 2), so the figures apply to both tests. Unequal variances would change Welch's power slightly.
- A 0–100 slider may pile up at the floor or ceiling. The simulation does not model that, so real power could be lower.
- 128 is the number of *completers*. Recruit a few more to allow for attention-check exclusions. The contingency money covers this.

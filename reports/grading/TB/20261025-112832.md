### C4. Statistical Analysis: level HD, 2/2 marks

- **Evidence:** "Welch's independent-samples t-test will compare mean ownership likelihood between groups. Unlike Student's t-test, it does not assume equal variances, which pessimistic framing may disturb by polarising responses." "The test assumes independent observations (one response per person, random allocation), interval-level measurement and approximately normal scores within each group; if floor or ceiling clustering makes scores non-normal, a Mann–Whitney U test, which compares rank distributions, will replace it." "H0 is rejected if p < .05; H1 is supported only if the pessimistic mean is lower." "Assuming a medium effect (d = 0.5), 64 per group gives 80% power."
- **Why this level:** The test choice follows from the number of groups, the design and the measurement level (R55). Interpretation is explicit: a decision rule, a two-sided test of a directional H1 with the reason, and a direction condition for supporting H1. Mean difference, 95% CI and Cohen's d are reported. The power figure is the standard result (d = 0.5, two-sided α = .05, 80% power gives 64 per group) and is consistent with heading 4. This is well above the HD threshold.
